# The satellite's identity is a whitelisted extract of preferences

**Date**: 2026-09-13 · **Work**: `satellites-plugin` · **Status**: active

## Context

A satellite has no identity of its own: it speaks with the mother's voice and adds a project role. It needs the mother's identity, tone, register and memory rules, and `preferences.md` also holds sections that must never reach a project repo's sessions (personal relationships, health, finances).

## Decision

A generator copies a fixed whitelist of `preferences.md` sections (`Identity`, `Owner — basics`, `Communication preferences`, `Writing register`) into one file in the mother's `private/`. Sections are matched by heading prefix, since real headings carry a suffix (`## Identity (the orchestrator)`, `## Identity (Client)`). The satellite's session-start hook regenerates it when `preferences.md` is newer than the extract, and injects it together with the `## Memory` and `## Writing register` blocks of the mother's `CLAUDE.md`, rewritten with absolute paths to the mother's `bin/mem` and `bin/register-check`: those rules live in `CLAUDE.md`, outside preferences. No model is involved. (Prefix matching and the `CLAUDE.md` blocks amended on 2026-09-14 after the impact review.)

## Alternatives discarded

- **Import `preferences.md` whole**: every satellite session would carry the owner's private sections.
- **Extract generated by a model**: output varies between runs, costs tokens at each refresh, and can paraphrase a private detail into the extract.
- **A copy written into each repo at install**: goes stale at the first change to preferences, once per satellite.

## Consequences

- A section outside the whitelist never leaves the mother, including sections added later.
- A new section that satellites should see requires changing the whitelist.
- The extract depends on heading prefixes: renaming a whitelisted heading in `preferences.md` silently drops it from the extract.
